BATUMI · FIRST PHRASES

Three Georgian words for your first day in Batumi

A SMALL PHRASEBOOK · BY DAIANNA

Batumi is a lovely place to start getting curious about Georgian. You’ll hear it all around you, written in its own beautiful alphabet. You don’t need a long phrasebook to begin: these three words can carry you through a few everyday moments.

გამარჯობაgamarjoba · hello

Use gamarjoba when you greet someone. It works in most everyday situations, whether you’re meeting a neighbour or stepping into a small shop. Say it warmly and give the other person a moment to respond.

მადლობაmadloba · thank you

Madloba is useful everywhere. You can say it after someone helps you, serves you, or holds a door. If you learn just one word before arriving, make it this one.

ნახვამდისnakhvamdis · goodbye

Nakhvamdis is a polite way to say goodbye. It may take a few tries to feel comfortable in your mouth, and that’s perfectly normal. Start slowly, listen, and try again.

About the pronunciation

The spellings above use Latin letters to give you a starting point. They can’t show every Georgian sound exactly, so listen to native speakers when you can. The Georgian script is consistent once you learn its letters; it’s well worth getting acquainted with it rather than relying on transliteration forever.

One small suggestion

Pick one phrase and use it once today. A word becomes yours by saying it to a real person, even if your pronunciation isn’t perfect yet. People usually appreciate the effort.

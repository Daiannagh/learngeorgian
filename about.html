A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I teach Georgian.
I’m still learning, too.

I’m Daianna: a native Georgian speaker, a teacher, and a language learner. I know the relief of having a grammar rule finally make sense.

MY STORY01 / 03

I know what it’s like when the rule just won’t click.

I speak Russian and I’m working through German. Learning another language keeps me close to the moments my students describe: understanding a rule in theory, then freezing when it’s time to use it.

That’s why I make space for questions, examples, and second tries. I want you to know why a sentence works—not just repeat it after me.

WHAT MATTERS IN A LESSON

Clear structure.
Room to be human.

I hold a Preply Language Teaching Certificate and have developed my own materials and exercises for people learning Georgian as a second language.

We’ll work steadily, but the plan is yours. Tell me what you need Georgian for, and I’ll tailor the lesson around that.

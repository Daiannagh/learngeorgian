HOW I TEACH

Why I teach the pattern, not just the phrase

When you’re learning a language, it’s easy to collect useful phrases and still feel unsure what to say next. I want you to see how Georgian sentences fit together, so you can begin making your own.

We take one idea at a time, look at examples, and practise using the pattern in a conversation. You don’t have to understand everything at once. A small “oh, I see” is a good step forward.

STARTING OUT

Your first words are allowed to be imperfect

It’s normal to want to get a new language exactly right before you speak. But speaking is part of how you learn it. We’ll practise slowly, make mistakes without fuss, and try again.

You don’t need to arrive knowing the alphabet or carrying a list of memorized phrases. Bring your curiosity and one thing you’d like to be able to say. We can start there.

WHAT A GROUP IS LIKEBATUMI · GEORGIA

Learn together.

We’ll work on the language as a group, but you’ll have time to try the words yourself, ask what you’re wondering, and hear how other learners approach the same challenge.

I use structured materials and practical examples. We’ll make room for the grammar, the pronunciation, and the conversations that matter in everyday life.

  • You’re living in Batumi or spending time here.

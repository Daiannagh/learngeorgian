HOW WE’LL WORK

A steady rhythm,
not a script.

We’ll start with what you already know and what you want Georgian to help you do. I’ll bring structure and materials; you can bring questions, interests, and the things that didn’t quite make sense last time.

Some lessons will be about learning something new. Others will be about practising until it feels more natural. We’ll adjust as you go.

02 A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m
Daianna.

I’m a native Georgian speaker, a teacher, and a language learner too.

I know what it’s like when a grammar rule just won’t click, or when you understand a lot but still can’t find the words. I’m learning German myself, and I bring that learner’s perspective into my lessons.

My teaching is structured, but never one-size-fits-all. I use materials and exercises I’ve made for people learning Georgian as a second language, then shape the lesson around what you want to do with it.

Lessons give you more than a list of phrases. You’ll build the tools to understand, remember, and use Georgian in your own life.
